# Synthetic charging geography evidence

All 170 retained owners remain represented. Group membership follows the saved parameter bank and Car profile records. The groups overlap: common_Car_profiles is a subset of common_energy_cases, and all_retained contains every group. Group counts must not be added as independent populations.

The four T codes are charging contexts, verified against the frequency, charger and start-window questions in the original survey snapshot. They are not automatically the four geographic place typologies. Routine non-home includes education and other places; destination includes restaurants and leisure; en-route does not prove motorway charging; home does not prove a suburban location.

Q20 records usual away-from-home geography. Q21 records a most-common outside district. They do not identify exact fractions of sessions, a destination separately for each context, or a session-level origin-destination matrix. District-pair rows count reporting owners only.

Canonical district names are matched using case and whitespace normalization only. Blank answers and unmatched text remain distinct. Unmatched text can be a valid non-district response and is not automatically missing data or an exclusion reason. A named outside district equal to home is retained as a review flag. No values are imputed or corrected.

These synthetic completeness cohorts do not establish national owner composition or travel behaviour. No routing probabilities, geographic place mappings, new eligibility rules, demand simulations, parameter draws or AI training are activated.
