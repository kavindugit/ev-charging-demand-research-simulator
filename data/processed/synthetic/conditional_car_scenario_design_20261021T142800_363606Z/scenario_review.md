# Proposed conditional Car scenario design

The published all-fuel motor-car count supplies the reference denominator. Shares and interpolation are proposed study assumptions. This package does not estimate the active EV fleet or fill the fleet input contract.

| Year | Low expected cars | Medium expected cars | High expected cars |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026 | 19,467.52 | 19,467.52 | 19,467.52 |
| 2030 | 32,445.87 | 54,076.44 | 97,337.60 |
| 2035 | 48,668.80 | 97,337.60 | 194,675.20 |

Counts retain six decimal places in the CSV. Fractions are expected scenario quantities; no integer fleet was generated. Low/medium/high label experimental share paths, not likelihoods or confidence limits.

The fixed denominator is a controlled reference scale, not a claim that the future national motor-car population stays constant. The 2025 newly registered Electric-car cohort is not used as EV stock.

Next decisions: review share endpoints and denominator policy; define and assess district allocation evidence/proxies and the behaviour-context to charging-place allocation. No district fleet counts, place weights, demand profiles or AI training are produced.
